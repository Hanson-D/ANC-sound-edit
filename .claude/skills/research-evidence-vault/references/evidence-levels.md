# Evidence Levels

Use these levels for patent, industry solution, product, and application records in any research project.

| Level | Meaning | Typical source |
|---|---|---|
| `official` | Public material from the patent office, assignee, manufacturer, standards body, support site, or product page. | Google Patents, vendor docs, support pages, standards pages |
| `measurement` | Third-party test, benchmark, dataset, or measurement page suitable as an evidence benchmark entry point. | Lab test pages, benchmark reports, measurement reviews |
| `review-derived` | Third-party review, media article, or industry-news summary. Useful as a lead, weaker than measurement. | Tom's Guide, AudioXpress, SoundGuys narrative review |
| `inferred` | Agent or seed-derived interpretation without a recognized official, patent, measurement, or review source. Requires manual upgrade before strong conclusions. | Placeholder discovery pointer, uncited product inference |

Prefer the most evidence-bearing level. For example, a third-party benchmark page should usually be `measurement`; a vendor claim should be `official`; a comparison article without measurement tables should be `review-derived`.

Do not convert qualitative product copy into numeric performance claims. Numeric claims need a measurement source and method context.
